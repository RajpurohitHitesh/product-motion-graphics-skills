# Cinematic / Futuristic

## Refined treatment: cinematic continuity and technical detail

The [faceless/motion comparison](../../references/video-studies/2026-11-14-craft-upgrade.md) contributes camera/overlay discipline and restrained Clean Tech labels. Apply [motion craft](../../references/motion-craft.md) without importing unrelated anatomy, cartoon characters or desktop chrome.

Text: use [a restrained reveal title, sparse technical labels and quiet brand/CTA hierarchy](../../references/heading-system.md), keeping printed product text untouched. Audio: use [a deliberate atmospheric arc and real material/reveal accents](../../references/sound-and-cursor.md); apply click/cursor choreography only to a genuine companion-app interaction.

- Give each camera move a product reason: reveal the silhouette, approach a real material/control, or connect to the hero. Carry the same visual landmark through related shots. Do not inherit the faceless engine's perpetual camera motion when a label needs a stable hold.
- Add at most one crisp technical callout attached to a real feature. In a 3D scene, project its anchor through the current camera; in a flat image, transform it with its source crop. Keep labels away from silhouettes, reflections and captions.
- A masked phrase or restrained accent line can introduce a detail, then yield to the material. Keep input typing and Software Walkthrough panels for a genuine companion-app beat only.
- Shape sound around the reveal and the actual detail, leaving room for narration. Use restrained scene changes instead of the Creator flash/glitch stack. Preserve real geometry and available viewing angles.
- Preview the reveal, a readable annotated detail and a stable hero. The [cover](../../references/thumbnails.md) needs enough light to identify the actual product at small size; a black silhouette without recognizable detail is not a finished cover.

Create a dramatic product reveal using controlled light, depth, material detail, and deliberate pacing. Futuristic styling should support a real product rather than disguise it.

## Visual system

- Base: near-black `#080B12`, silver-white `#E8EDF5`, and one brand-compatible cool accent such as cyan `#54D9FF` or violet `#9575FF`.
- Use precise sans typography and restrained tracking. Keep smaller supporting text solid and readable rather than glowing.
- Establish a clear key light and stable product silhouette. Layer atmosphere, background gradients, or a few particles behind the product.
- Reserve bloom and reflections for edges or materials where they make visual sense. Do not tint packaging text, screens, or logos until they lose their actual colors.

## Motion grammar at 30 fps

- Reveal: 24–45 frames, allowing a silhouette to become a fully readable product.
- Detail motion: slow virtual camera drift or 2–6% scaling across a shot. Keep foreground/background motion coherent.
- Accent reveals: 12–24 frame restrained sweeps or masks; avoid repetitive sci-fi HUD overlays unrelated to the product.
- Use depth through layered planes and shadows. A real 3D rotation requires a model or suitable multi-angle footage; a single photograph is not enough to invent an accurate back or side.
- Use deliberate contrast between a longer reveal and a shorter feature beat, ending on a stable hero shot.

## Product story: adaptable 30-second example

| Time | Purpose | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Hook | Product silhouette or a truthful feature close-up |
| 3–10s | Reveal | Controlled light exposes the actual product |
| 10–22s | Features | Two or three material, function, or UI details |
| 22–27s | Hero | Full product and strongest supported promise |
| 27–30s | CTA | Clean, readable finish with reduced effects |

## Inputs and female voice

Use supplied detail footage, real product cutouts, or approved 3D assets. For a single photo, use a 2D reveal and layered background; do not simulate unsupported product geometry. Treat generative image-to-video as a separate optional capability and check product consistency if it is available.

Voice: composed, confident female delivery with natural pauses, roughly 115–140 words/minute. Use subtle atmospheric music; keep rises and impacts away from important words.

## Preview and quality checks

Show a light reveal, a readable product detail, and one cinematic transition with narration. Check that dark areas retain detail, product edges are clean, lighting does not flicker, and the viewer can identify the product before the preview ends.

## Reference improvements

### Optional variation: material macro and exploded detail

Plan texture close-up → control/detail → separated components → whole-product hero. Use soft warm directional light when it suits the real material; cool neon is optional. Component separation requires genuine layered/3D assets or suitable footage. Storyboards establish the shot concept, not an orbit path or easing curve. See the [headphone reference study](references/2026-11-14-headphones-material-reveal.md).

Save observed lighting recipes, depth relationships, and reveal timings. Keep an optional effect separate from a genuinely different visual style. Do not assume a reference used 3D or a particular AI tool without evidence. See [reference learning](../../references/reference-learning.md).

### Optional variation: cool beauty packshot

Use a product-colored cool stage, generous space, and soft directional light around a real cosmetic/container hero. Keep packaging legible and surfaces believable. Water/gel effects are optional production choices requiring suitable assets, not measured observations from the small samples. See the [skincare study](references/2026-11-14-cool-beauty-packshot.md).

### Optional variation: neon pedestal

Frame a genuine object silhouette on a stepped platform, with restrained edge accents and a clear final product view. Keep neon behind or around important labels. Use this staging when it suits the product, rather than applying a hardware podium to every app. See the [launch-stage study](references/2026-11-14-neon-pedestal.md). Exact outline animation and camera movement remain unverified.
