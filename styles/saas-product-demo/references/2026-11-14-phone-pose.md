# Controlled phone pose

Source: [phone animation reference](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/RmT13ED-enA), reported 9s. Reviewed 2026-11-14 through [48 × 27-per-tile samples](../../../references/video-studies/evidence/2026-11-14-batch-04/RmT13ED-enA-overview-0.jpg) and a [larger poster](../../../references/video-studies/evidence/2026-11-14-batch-04/RmT13ED-enA-poster.jpg). No continuous playback/audio.

Across ~0–9s, sampled states show a device at differing orientations on a dark field. The poster separately clarifies an angled phone, a violet concentric-ring backdrop, and an example channel screen. Fine UI detail, rotation axis, trajectory, and speed are unverified.

Match: **SaaS / Product Demo**, optional phone-pose treatment. Use the user's real app screen in an appropriate device frame, then settle toward a readable view. Keep decorative rings subordinate and avoid making legibility depend on pausing a tilted screen. A suitable 2.5D treatment can be designed without claiming the source's implementation was reproduced.
