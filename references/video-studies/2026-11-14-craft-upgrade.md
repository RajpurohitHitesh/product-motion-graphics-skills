## Reference runtime checks

The extracted examples were checked in the local browser, without changing the archived source prompts. Selected frames were inspected at Creator 8.8/15.6s, Clean Tech 1.8/7.8/14.1s and Walkthrough 11.8/16.6s. Repeated seeking matched at Creator 15.6s and Clean Tech 14.1s. Walkthrough initially differed; after an additional font-readiness barrier, checks at 1.5/5.8/11.8/16.6/18.5s matched. The first mismatch's exact cause was not isolated, so capture guidance requires explicit font/image readiness and a repeat-frame check rather than assuming the player flag is sufficient.

Offline synthesized scores rendered at a diagnostic 12 kHz with finite stereo samples and the expected durations: Creator 21.5s (258,000 frames), Clean Tech 20s (240,000), Walkthrough 19.5s (234,000). This tests execution/buffer shape only; it is not a listening review or a production-quality audio export.

## Heading, music and cursor refinement

Following the owner's emphasis on detailed titles, supporting copy, background music, clicks and cursor polish, the walkthrough's title at 1.5s and checked closing lower third at 18.5s were also inspected locally. Its small topic label, large two-line task title, metadata pill, numbered step label and subdued supporting text were compared with Creator's display/serif/body hierarchy and Clean Tech's statement/card/mono-label hierarchy. This adds [a complete heading system](../heading-system.md), linked from all ten styles, and expands the three adaptation prompts and brief fields.

[Music and cursor guidance](../sound-and-cursor.md) now distinguishes each treatment's bed/phrase shape, tactile effect roles, speech-aware mix and intentional pointer/touch sequence. It specifies that a ripple stays anchored to the event-time target while the pointer leaves. Sound direction remains code-derived until an actual new mix is listened to. These refinements do not assert that a new video or sound mix has been produced or auditioned.
