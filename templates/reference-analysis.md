## Classification

Record the matched existing style or the new style name. Compare the nearest alternatives and explain what makes an effect a variation or a distinct visual language.

## Reusable improvements

Describe actionable techniques, timing/parameter starting points, suitable product contexts, and boundaries. Include only what was actually learned; do not carry over the reference's product claims or imply use of its media assets.

## Changes and verification

List the guide/menu changes and link them with relative paths. Mark each technique as observed, implemented in a video, or preview-validated, according to what actually happened. Record remaining uncertainty.
